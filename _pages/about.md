Hi! I’m Junyi ~
======s
I am now a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Washington University in St. Louis (WashU). Previously, I worked as a Research Scientist intern at Meta Reality Labs (formerly Facebook).

My research interests span various Soft/Flexible/Stretchable/Wearable Electronics:

- Flexible/Stretchable Optoelectronic Devices (perovskite-based light-emitting diodes, photodetectors, solar cells, lasers, etc.)
- Soft sensors & Medical-grade Wearable Electronic Systems for real-time health monitoring in everyday and clinical contexts
- Human-Computer Interactions (HCI): input devices, haptics display, human-machine interface
- Neurological Micro-/Nano-electronics.
