I am a **Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University**. I received my **Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering** from **Washington University in St. Louis (WashU)**. Previously, I worked as a **Research Scientist intern at Meta Reality Labs**.  

My research interests span various **soft / flexible / stretchable / wearable electronics**, including:

1. **Flexible/stretchable optoelectronic devices**  
   (perovskite-based light-emitting diodes, photodetectors, solar cells, lasers, and related systems)

2. **Soft sensors and medical-grade wearable electronic systems**  
   for real-time health monitoring in both everyday and clinical contexts

3. **Human–computer interactions (HCI)**  
   including input devices, haptic displays, and human–machine interfaces

4. **Neurological micro-/nano-electronics**
