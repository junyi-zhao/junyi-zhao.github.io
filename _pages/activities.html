Outreach

BrightPath STEAM Academy Summer Camp — Jul 2023

BrightPath STEAM Academy works to increase Black/African American youth representation and exposure to STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Math), promoting educational equity and expanding access to future opportunities in science and engineering.

Praxis Week — Jun 2023

During Praxis Week , students from John Burroughs School interacted with professional scientists, engineers, and physicians to explore career pathways in fields including data science, biotechnology, engineering, and robotics.

I participated as a STEM mentor, sharing my research experience and engaging students in discussions about science, engineering, and future career opportunities.

BrightPath STEAM Academy Summer Camp — Jul 2022

Participated in the 2022 BrightPath STEAM Academy Summer Camp to engage underrepresented youth in science, technology, engineering, arts, and mathematics.
